What areas do you support?

We mainly support Osaka city and the Kansai area, and can also work through partner networks across Japan depending on the project. Please tell us the facility location and room count first.

How many days in advance should I request?

It depends on the content and area, but earlier consultation is recommended. For urgent cleaning or busy seasons, please contact us on LINE first.

Do you provide photo reports?

Yes. We emphasize sharing post-cleaning photos so managers can check remotely. The reporting method can be adjusted to each site.
